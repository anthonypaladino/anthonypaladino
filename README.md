# Anthony Paladino 

I'm Anthony Paladino, a strategic alliance and cloud partnership executive based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. I've spent twenty-plus years building the partnerships that bring security and cloud technology to market — not managing relationships, but building the programs, products, and go-to-market models from scratch. This page is the story of how I got here.

## Security Foundations

I started at the edge of the network, not the boardroom. My first years were as a Security Solutions Architect — closing federal deployments of Wireless IDS systems into the Department of State and the Pentagon, training engineers in Wi-Fi attack and defense, and spending a decade attending DEFCon and Black Hat as a presenter and panelist rather than a spectator. That hands-on foundation shapes how I approach every partnership I build: I understand what I'm selling, and I understand why it matters to the customer on the other side of the table.

## Cloud Wi-Fi & Security Infrastructure

In 2005 I joined Mojo Networks (formerly AirTight Networks) in a hands-on role that spanned the field and product development, and went on to become VP of OEM and Strategic Partnerships. What followed was thirteen years of building — the kind of building that starts with a blank page and ends with something that outlasts the company that made it.

We started as a pure security play: wireless intrusion prevention (WIPS). I helped develop the Wi-Fi integration and led the move to a single hybrid unit. Compliance was driving the opportunities, PCI-DSS above all, and many retailers couldn't afford a dedicated point solution. They needed best-in-class WIPS as a security overlay, with one radio dedicated to security and another dedicated to Wi-Fi, in the same device. That combined product is what everything after it was built on.

The defining project was Wi-Fi Pro, a cloud-managed enterprise Wi-Fi platform I built with Comcast from the ground up. No product existed. No revenue model existed. I architected the underlying super-tenant (manager-of-manager) infrastructure, wrote the GTM playbook, and negotiated the OEM deal. First-year revenue came in well ahead of targets, the product grew month over month, and it became the company's largest revenue driver. The platform remains in active national deployment today — years after I left.

Comcast's investment in the product included something I'm still proud of: the first national television ad campaign ever run for a Wi-Fi product, featuring actor Titus Welliver (*Bosch*). A few of the spots: <a href="https://www.ispot.tv/ad/AqIR/comcast-business-wifi-pro-hotcakes" target="_blank">Hotcakes</a> · <a href="https://www.ispot.tv/ad/AWCu/comcast-business-phones-before-forks" target="_blank">Phones Before Forks</a> · <a href="https://www.ispot.tv/ad/7Jl2/comcast-business-wifi-anywhere" target="_blank">WiFi Anywhere</a> · <a href="https://www.ispot.tv/ad/7DKI/comcast-business-always-choose-the-fastest" target="_blank">Always Choose the Fastest</a>.

Beyond Comcast, I negotiated and launched OEM partnerships with Google (Google Station), Facebook (Express Wi-Fi / Free Basics across Africa), WatchGuard Technologies (Total Secure Wi-Fi, a custom-built cloud security product for their EMEA and APJ markets), and NEC Platforms in Tokyo. Each one started with a conversation and ended with a shipping product and recurring revenue.

My work has never been confined to one region. In 2019 I moved to Comcast Business as Senior Director of Enterprise Solutions, where I managed a large enterprise revenue portfolio and ran co-sell relationships with leading security ISV partners — Palo Alto Networks, Fortinet, Cisco, HP-Aruba, and Juniper-Mist. ## AWS

In 2022 I joined Amazon Web Services. I've spent the past four years running partner programs at the center of how AWS engages its most strategic security ISV ecosystem.

As WW Strategy Lead for the AWS Built-In program, I created the AWS Built-In Cybersecurity ISV Competency — a co-build validation program that worked hands-on with partners to automate deployment using AWS-verified modular code integrations. I managed a portfolio of strategic security partners, with CrowdStrike as the flagship, and built the joint business planning, QBR, and field alignment model from scratch.

Since mid-2024 I've been running partner GTM for AWS's Applied AI Solutions team. I built the partner funding program from zero — wrote the rules of engagement and structured the intake and funding model that helps partners deliver AI solutions to customers faster. It is the same kind of work I've always done: figure out what doesn't exist yet, build it, make it repeatable.
